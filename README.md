# Supplementary Material

**Manuscript:** α-Glucosidase Inhibitory Potential of *Cladonia scabriuscula* Extract in Caco-2 Cells with UHPLC-HRMS Metabolite Profiling and In Silico Molecular Docking

**Journal:** Research in Pharmaceutical Sciences (RPS)

This repository contains the supplementary material accompanying the manuscript. It is provided to support the peer-review process and to ensure long-term, openly accessible archiving of the supporting data.

### Figures S1–S19

**Fig. S19** shows the UHPLC–HRMS base peak chromatograms of the two extracts. **Figs. S1–S18** show the high-resolution mass spectra (negative ion mode, M–H⁻) of the 18 metabolites annotated in the ethyl acetate (ECS) and methanol (MCS) extracts of *C. scabriuscula*:

- **Fig. S1** Azelaic acid
- **Fig. S2** Undecanedioic acid
- **Fig. S3** D-Glucitol monostearate
- **Fig. S4** Icosanedioic acid bis(2,3-dihydroxypropyl) ester
- **Fig. S5** 9Z-Octadecenedioic acid
- **Fig. S6** 13-(β-D-Glucosyloxy)docosanoic acid
- **Fig. S7** Portentol
- **Fig. S8** 9-Oxooctadecanoic acid
- **Fig. S9** Heptadecyl D-glucoside
- **Fig. S10** allo-Protolichestrinic acid
- **Fig. S11** Linderanolide
- **Fig. S12** 3,5-Dimethylorsellinic acid

- **Fig. S18** Homosekikaic acid

### Table S1

Top-ranked docking pose of each identified metabolite and acarbose against sucrase-isomaltase (PDB 3LPP), including binding affinity (kcal/mol) and the hydrophilic and hydrophobic residues involved.
